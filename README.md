# E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, profit, orders, products, regions, categories, and customer segments.

The dashboard helps identify key business trends and supports data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify the best-performing product categories
- Compare regional sales performance
- Analyze customer segment performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Track sales trends over time
- Provide interactive filtering using Year and Category

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- SQL Server
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV Dataset

- Profit by Segment
- Sales Trend Over Time
- Top 10 Products by Sales

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Technology** generated the highest sales at approximately **$836.15K**.
2. **Technology** was also the most profitable category with approximately **$145.45K** profit.
3. **Furniture** generated approximately **$742K** in sales but only **$18.45K** profit, indicating relatively weak profitability.
4. The **West** region generated the highest sales at approximately **$725.46K**, while the **South** region had the lowest at approximately **$391.72K**.
5. The **Consumer** segment contributed approximately **$1.16M**, representing about **50.5% of total sales**.
6. **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the standout product among the Top 10 products, generating approximately **$62K** in sales.
